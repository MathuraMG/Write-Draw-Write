How to Play

  1. Everyone joins the same room on their phone.
  2. Each player is secretly given a word to draw.
  3. You have 30 seconds — draw as best you can!
  4. Your drawing is passed to the next player, who must guess the word in 30 seconds.
  5. That guess is passed on for someone else to draw again.
  6. This continues until every chain has gone through all players.
  7. Then comes The Reveal — see how your original word transformed!

No winners. Just chaos and laughter.
